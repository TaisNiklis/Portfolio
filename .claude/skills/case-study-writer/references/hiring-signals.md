# Hiring signals

## How a case is read
- **Recruiter, 30–60 s:** title, metrics in the header, TL;DR, headings. Decides "relevant or not".
- **Design lead / hiring manager, 3–5 min:** problem framing, decisions, trade-offs, impact.
  Decides on "seniority".
- **Portfolio review interview:** the case is the script. Every claim must be explainable live.

## The 6 things every case must prove
1. **Ownership:** what *she* did, not only the team ("I led…", "I proposed…", "I convinced…").
2. **Product thinking:** understands the business problem and model, not only the UI.
3. **Decisions:** options considered, why one won, what was traded off.
4. **Collaboration:** PMs, engineers, clients, stakeholders, conflicting requirements.
5. **Outcome:** metrics, or clear proxy evidence with a stated source.
6. **Target skills:** B2B complexity, AI features, design systems. These are the filters the
   target companies use.
